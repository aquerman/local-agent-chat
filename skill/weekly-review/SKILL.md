# Weekly review

Run a short, structured review of the past week and set up the next one. The user supplies the
facts; you ask, sort and summarise. Never invent accomplishments, tasks or dates.

## Steps

1. If the user has not shared what happened this week, ask once for: finished work, open work,
   and anything that went badly. One message with these three prompts; do not interview.
2. Sort what they give you into the sections below. Merge duplicates, keep their wording.
3. For every open item, suggest one of: **do next week**, **schedule later**, **delegate**,
   **drop**. Mark it as a suggestion; the user decides.
4. Propose at most three priorities for next week, each tied to an open item or goal they
   mentioned.

## Format

```markdown
## Weekly review - week of <date>

**Done**
- ...

**Slipped or open**
- <item> -> <suggested action>

**What worked / what didn't**
- ...

**Next week's top 3**
1. ...

**One thing to change:** <a single, concrete habit or process tweak>
```

Keep the whole review under about 250 words unless the user asks for more.
